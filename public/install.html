About this app

Every chat. One inbox. Answered by AI.

Tectobox unifies your WhatsApp, Telegram and website chat into one beautiful inbox. Your AI answers customers instantly — trained on your real products and prices. You step in only when it matters.

Get notified the second a customer writes, track hot leads with AI scores, and hand any chat to a human with one tap.

What's new

  • Push notifications — new messages reach you even with the app closed
  • Simple human check on the website chat widget
  • Automatic updates — you're always on the latest version
  • Fresh new look, faster everywhere

Data safety

Sessions are encrypted. Your workspace data is private to you — the app never shares it. No ads, no trackers.
